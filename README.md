# Mount Elizabeth Dental Surgery — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Mount Elizabeth Dental Surgery is a Singapore-based multi-specialty dental practice established in 1986, located at Mount Elizabeth Medical Centre in Orchard. The clinic provides specialist-led dental care across prosthodontics, orthodontics, oral and maxillofacial surgery, children's dentistry, endodontics, and other dental specialties.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the practice's specialist expertise, simplifying treatment discovery, and strengthening patient confidence through a professional, informative, and appointment-focused digital experience.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Specialist-Focused Hero Messaging

**Previous Issue:** The original homepage did not communicate the clinic's specialist-led approach, professional expertise, and key differentiators clearly enough at first glance.

**Redesign Improvement:** Introduced a stronger hero message highlighting specialist dental care, supported by a concise description of the practice and prominent appointment and call actions. The improved introduction helps visitors immediately understand the clinic's positioning and available consultation pathways.

### 2. Clearer Dental Specialty Navigation

**Previous Issue:** Although the clinic offers a broad range of dental specialties, the original homepage did not provide a sufficiently direct and intuitive way for visitors to identify relevant treatments.

**Redesign Improvement:** Organised the main dental specialties into distinct visual service cards featuring relevant imagery, concise descriptions, and direct treatment links. This structured presentation makes specialist services easier to scan, compare, and explore without unnecessary navigation.

### 3. Improved Trust Building and Appointment Accessibility

**Previous Issue:** Important information about the clinic's specialist credentials, established history, location, and contact options was not sufficiently integrated into a connected patient journey.

**Redesign Improvement:** Brought specialist profiles, professional qualifications, the practice's history since 1986, Mount Elizabeth Medical Centre location details, and prominent appointment options into a more cohesive homepage structure. These improvements help patients evaluate the practice and access consultation information more conveniently.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several targeted visual and usability enhancements:

- **Stronger Visual Hierarchy:** Established a clearer progression from specialist-focused messaging to treatments, professional expertise, and appointment actions.
- **Improved Specialty Discovery:** Presented dental specialties through structured visual cards with accessible treatment information.
- **Professional Visual Presentation:** Emphasised a clean, organised design reflecting the clinic's specialist healthcare positioning.
- **Enhanced Trust Communication:** Increased the visibility of specialist credentials, practitioner information, and the practice's established history.
- **Clearer Appointment Pathways:** Positioned appointment and telephone contact options more prominently throughout the homepage.
- **Consistent User Experience:** Improved content organisation, readability, and navigation to create a more cohesive patient journey.

## Summary

The redesigned Mount Elizabeth Dental Surgery homepage addresses three primary challenges: weak specialist-focused messaging, unclear specialty navigation, and insufficient integration of trust-building information with appointment opportunities.

The redesign establishes a more informative, professional, and patient-focused experience that highlights the clinic's specialist expertise, makes treatments easier to discover, and provides clearer pathways towards consultation and appointment enquiries.
